🏢 Attrition by Department

Shows which departments have the highest employee turnover. R&D leads with 133 departures (56%), followed by Sales with 92 (39%).

💡 Key Insight: R&D department requires immediate attention with 133 departures, representing 56.1% of total attrition.

👤 Attrition by Age Group

Middle-aged employees (26-35) show highest attrition with 116 departures (49% of total), while younger employees (18-25) account for 40 departures (17%).

💡 Key Insight: Focus on mentorship programs for employees under 35 to improve retention.

⏰ Overtime Impact on Attrition

Employees working overtime show 3x higher attrition. Work-life balance is a critical retention factor.

💡 Key Insight: Reducing overtime could prevent up to 30% of attrition cases.

⚖️ Work-Life Balance Distribution

Majority of employees rate work-life balance as "Better" (3/4), but 29% report poor balance (ratings 1-2) requiring intervention.

💡 Key Insight: Target employees with rating 1-2 for immediate support and flexible work options.

👥 About This Project

A comprehensive HR Analytics platform developed for the MIT Emerging Talent program, combining machine learning, cloud infrastructure, and interactive dashboards to transform employee data into actionable insights.

🎯 Project Overview

The HR Analytics project is developed as part of the MIT Emerging Talent ELO-2 module, focusing on data-driven insights and machine learning applications in human resources management.

📊 Dataset Information

IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition & Performance Dataset

1,470 Employee Records
35 Attributes
16.1% Attrition Rate
0 Missing Values

The dataset includes comprehensive employee information covering demographics, job details, compensation, satisfaction metrics, and performance indicators. It's used to identify patterns and predictors of employee attrition.

🔍 Key Findings

1

Overtime Effect

Employees working overtime have 3× higher attrition rate (30.5%) compared to those without overtime (10.4%)

2

Age Correlation

Young employees aged 18-25 show highest attrition at ~35%, decreasing with age to 8% for 55+ group

3

Department Impact

R&D department has the highest number of departures with 133 employees (56%), followed by Sales with 92 (39%) and HR with 12 (5%)

4

Travel Frequency

Frequent business travelers face 24.9% attrition risk, significantly higher than non-travelers (8%)
