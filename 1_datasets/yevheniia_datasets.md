# HR Analytics Datasets List

This document provides a list of datasets that can be used for an HR Analytics project, including real links and short descriptions.

---

## 1. IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition & Performance

**Link:** <https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/pavansubhasht/ibm-hr-analytics-attrition-dataset>

**Description:**
One of the most widely used HR datasets. Includes employee satisfaction, compensation, performance, job roles, and whether the employee left (attrition). Ideal for predictive modeling.

**Common Metrics:**

- Attrition rate
- Job satisfaction index
- Average years at company
- Performance rating distribution
- Work-life balance score

⸻

---

## 2. HR Analytics: Job Change Prediction

**Link:** <https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/arashnic/hr-analytics-job-change-of-data-scientists>

**Description:**
Predict whether a candidate is looking for a new job. Contains education, training, experience, and demographics. Useful for recruitment analytics.

**Common Metrics:**

- Job change likelihood
- Candidate experience level distribution
- Training enrollment rate
- Education level segmentation

---

## 3. Employee Retention Dataset

**Link:** <https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/giripujar/hr-analytics>

**Description:**
Features satisfaction, performance score, hours worked, promotions, and whether the employee stayed. Good for retention analytics and visualizations.

**Common Metrics:**

- Satisfaction score
- Performance rating
- Promotion frequency
- Monthly working hours trends

---

## 4. Glassdoor Salary & Reviews Data

**Link:** <https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/joncshr/employee-reviews>

**Description:**
Includes employee reviews, company ratings, job titles, and pros/cons. Useful for culture, compensation, and employer branding analytics.

**Common Metrics:**

- Average company rating
- Work-life balance rating
- Recommendation score
- Sentiment scores from reviews

---

## 5. Indeed Job Posting Data

**Link:** <https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/shivamb/indeed-job-postings>

**Description:**
Job postings with titles, descriptions, skills, and salaries (when provided). Excellent for talent acquisition and workforce planning insights.

**Common Metrics:**

- Skill frequency
- Salary averages by role
- Demand for job categories
- Posting-to-hire ratio

---

## 6. Employee Engagement & Satisfaction Surveys

**Link:** <https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/ahsan81/hotel-reservations-classification-dataset> (example survey structure)

**Description:**
Survey-based datasets often include engagement levels, satisfaction scores, and workplace perceptions. Great for sentiment and cultural analysis.

---

## 7. LinkedIn Career Path & Skills Demand Data

**Link:** <https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/iamsouravbanerjee/linkedin-job-postings>

**Description:**
Dataset inspired by LinkedIn job listings: skills, roles, industries, and descriptions. Useful for skill demand analysis and career path research.

---

## 8. Synthetic HRIS (HR Information System) Data

**Link:** <https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/rhuebner/human-resources-data-set>

**Description:**
Simulated HRIS employee records: salary, demographics, departments, performance, and job history. Perfect for dashboarding and HRIS-like analysis.

---

## 9. Diversity & Inclusion Metrics Data

**Link:** <https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/mysarahmadbhat/diversity-in-tech>

**Description:**
DEI dataset showing gender and demographic representation across tech roles. Useful for pay equity, diversity analysis, and inclusive hiring.

---

## 10. Recruitment Funnel Datasets

**Link:** <https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/arnabbiswas1/hr-data-set>

**Description:**
Contains recruitment pipeline data: sourcing, screening, offers, and drop-offs. Perfect for funnel optimization and recruiter performance analytics.
